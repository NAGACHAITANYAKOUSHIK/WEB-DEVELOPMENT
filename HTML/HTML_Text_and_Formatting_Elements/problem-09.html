Student Terms & Rules

This page contains the important terms, rules, and notices that every student must follow.

General Rules

1. Students must maintain discipline and proper behavior inside the institution.

2. Students must carry their valid student ID card while attending classes.

3. Students must keep classrooms and campus premises clean.

Attendance

4. Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in all subjects.

Attendance below the required percentage may result in students being restricted from examinations.

Medical or other approved reasons may be considered according to institutional rules.

Examination Rules

5. Students must carry their examination hall ticket and valid ID card.

6. Mobile phones and unauthorized electronic devices are not allowed inside the examination hall.

Using unfair means during an examination may result in disciplinary action.

Assignment Rules

7. All assignments must be submitted before the given deadline.

Late submission may result in marks being deducted.

Assignment Deadline: 30 September 2026
Students should submit their work through the prescribed submission method.

Library Rules

8. Students must return borrowed books on or before the due date.

Late return of books may result in a fine.

Important Notice

Students are responsible for following all institutional rules and regulations.

Rules may be updated by the institution whenever necessary. Students should regularly check official notices.
