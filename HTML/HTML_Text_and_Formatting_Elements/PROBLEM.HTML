ABOUT THE COLLEGE

GLA University is a private university located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, India. It was established in 1998 and is known for its focus on engineering and technology education.

CAMPUS LIFE

Campus life at GLA University includes academic activities, sports, cultural events, technical competitions, clubs, seminars, workshops, and other student activities. Students get opportunities to interact with their classmates and develop teamwork and leadership skills. The university campus provides facilities for students to study, participate in activities, and build their professional skills.

WHY I LIKE MY COLLEGE

I like GLA University because it provides a good environment for learning and personal growth. The university has supportive teachers, modern classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, a library, sports facilities, and various extracurricular activities. I also like the campus atmosphere because it gives students opportunities to make friends, participate in events, and develop their skills. Overall, GLA University helps students gain knowledge, confidence, and practical skills for their future careers.
